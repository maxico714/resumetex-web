Every score below is measured by the local ATS analyser on a complete sample CV.
The ATS-safe set is single-column, table-free and uses standard section names, so parsers read it in the order you wrote it. The creative set looks better to a human but costs parseability — the score tells you how much.

How the score is calculated

100 points, computed locally in your browser/server — no CV is uploaded to score a template.
